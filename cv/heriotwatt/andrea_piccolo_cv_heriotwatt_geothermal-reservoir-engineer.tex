%     PROFILE STATEMENT
% ============================================================

\vspace{6pt}
\small{Computational geoscientist and Python/Julia developer with a PhD in Geodynamics and a decade of experience building coupled thermo-mechanical and basin-scale numerical models across four research institutions. Extended an open-source Julia modelling package to merge forward mechanical processes into basin forward modelling, and have built and optimised subsurface simulation software (FEniCSx, finite element/finite difference methods) achieving up to a 10\texttimes{} speedup. Looking to bring this coupled-physics modelling background and rapid uptake of new scientific software to reservoir and well engineering challenges in geothermal energy.}
